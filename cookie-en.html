How cookies are used on this portfolio.

Necessary storage

The website uses technical storage to work and remember your choices. demian_buscatti_cookie_consent_v1 stores acceptance or refusal of statistics, the notice version and the choice date, for up to 180 days. Session keys demian_intro_seen and demian_language_navigation manage the introduction and language navigation.

Optional statistics: Google Analytics

Only after accepting statistics, this website loads Google Analytics 4, property G-Z3J601SW05, provided by Google, to measure visits and website usage. Analytics can process online identifiers, device and browser information, visited pages and interactions. Cookies include _ga and _ga_*, configured by this website to expire after 180 days without renewing that expiry on visits. This implementation does not enable advertising storage or personalised advertising.

Consent and refusal

Analytics is blocked before your choice and when you refuse optional cookies. Necessary technical storage does not depend on accepting statistics. Refusal does not prevent browsing or contacting me. Consent is the basis for optional statistics (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR); the Italian Privacy Code rules on storing or accessing information on a device also apply.

Changing or withdrawing your choice

Use Cookie preferences in the footer of any page to accept or refuse statistics. Withdrawal disables Analytics, removes accessible first-party Analytics cookies and reloads the page if the tag was already running. It does not delete data previously collected by Google. Your choice is requested again after 180 days or a material notice update. You can also clear cookies and local storage in your browser. If storage is unavailable, your choice only applies to the current page.

Providers and further information

Google Analytics data retention depends on the property settings and is distinct from cookie expiry. See the Privacy Policy, Google Privacy Policy and Analytics cookie documentation. Google Fonts loads fonts independently of optional Analytics. Third-party sites opened through links have their own policies.
